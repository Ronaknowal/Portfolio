## Useful applications and scope decisions

- OS: numerical workers show how results cross process boundaries; private/shared mappings explain why isolated worker state and shared external effects are different. Fork/exec/wait were added during writing because distinguishing identity, image replacement and status collection belongs here. Current title remains accurate.
- Arrays: normalized label counting exposes a real text-equality pitfall; the inverted index combines ordered documents, map lookup and posting sets. It deliberately does not claim language-aware tokenization, stable IDs after reordering or a complete search engine.
- Linked structures: LRU shows why keyed lookup and recency complement each other; the moving mean turns FIFO membership into a streaming calculation. The queue explicitly rejects full writes; bounded history eviction is a different policy. Neither example claims production concurrency or cache-validity coverage.
- The existing Hashing topic owns formal collision/amortization analysis; a [growth/shrink threshold note](docs/teaching/topic-notes/hashing-collision-resolution-amortized-analysis.md) proposes a useful counterexample. The existing Caching Strategies lesson owns production policy; a [validity/workload note](docs/teaching/topic-notes/caching-strategies-semantic-exact-kv-cache-sharing.md) records the new bridge and specific unqualified claims found during a scoped source check. Both notes remain open for the destination's author, and their lessons were not rewritten. No catalogue-wide audit was performed.

All three current titles fit the final scope; no IDs, display titles or module assignments were changed. Linked Lists now explicitly requires Arrays and OOP. This introduces Arrays into the Embodied Intelligence route and Arrays/OOP into Research & Revision before their dependent lesson. Every previous path topic remains in its previous relative order; the other five routes, including the opening full curriculum, are unchanged.
