## Completion evidence contract

For every scoped topic, inspect and record:

1. Its actual original material and saved notes; observable outcomes, coverage retained/repaired/extended, title decision, core/deeper progression and the next module topic.
2. Primary-source verification of the particular claims, conventions and current interfaces; annotated useful written and video alternatives with the actual reviewed scope.
3. Complete first-time explanations, justified derivations or correctness arguments, worked values/state, original relevant applications, and independent practice with hints and explained feedback. DSA also needs verified official LeetCode selections, transfer tasks and an updated cross-topic coverage map.
4. Concept-specific inline diagrams and investigations as needed, with actual model/data provenance, accurate labels and values, clear boundaries and bounded browser computation. A generic container or lab count does not prove adequacy.
5. Appropriate independent mathematical/native/model evidence, including meaningful counterexamples and boundaries. Confirm displayed outputs and quantities rather than only duplicating the implementation in tests.
6. Actual desktop/narrow-screen and keyboard behavior; opened screenshots for visual inspection, ordinary reading-flow review, valid anchors and links, state/reset/error behavior, readable diagrams and mathematical rendering where used.
7. Source ownership, lazy loading, stable IDs/module order/progress, publication conservation, fresh generated artifacts, curriculum verification, production build and relevant integrated browser/loading checks.

Only final evidence supports an implementation-reviewed status. For future scoped revisions, record unresolved concerns honestly and complete their required checks. User approval and observed beginner learning remain distinct from author review and cannot be inferred from tests.
