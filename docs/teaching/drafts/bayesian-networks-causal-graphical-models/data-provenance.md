License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). UCI DOI page, dimensions and license inspected 12 September 2026. This is cultivar classification, not taste, quality, intervention or clinical outcome data.

Unknown measurement units are not invented.
