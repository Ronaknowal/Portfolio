Consequential repairs: FFT output 0 wraps h1×last-input, not h_last×last-input; next power of two is not generally 2L; floating-point zero differences are not guaranteed. A causal padding scheme can still fail prefix consistency if position coordinates rescale with active input length. Gates need not be sigmoids or probabilities; a conditional mixing matrix is not the full network Jacobian. Deep ungated networks are not generically unable to copy, and a fixed-delay task is solved by a plain shift filter. Short causal preprocessing is not RoPE. Implicit parameters do not remove coefficient-generation/storage cost or guarantee extrapolation. A decay window is not a mathematical necessity for a finite FIR. No fixed recursion depth universally makes a model unstable. An attention layer in a hybrid does not take over the convolution layers' incremental updates. Modal approximation must be checked rather than declared automatically exact. Unsupported timing curves, fake enhancer-accuracy curves, global “Mamba wins/Hyena is the only credible model” claims and incorrect historical genealogy were removed.
