### The radical defect — wrong mathematics, not wrong presentation

**Every square root on the page was missing its radical.** Section 4 rendered

    r_K = ln(2K/δ) / (2n)

and section 7 rendered `r = (32/n)(d ln(en/d) + ln(8/δ))`. Those are the radius **squared** — a different
quantity. A learner reading section 4 and solving for a sample size would get n wrong by an order of magnitude.
This is the first defect in this effort that changed what the page *asserted* rather than how it looked.

The cause is worth stating exactly, because it generalises to any lesson in this repository:

> A lesson stylesheet's `.pac-lesson svg { width: 100%; height: auto }` — written to lay out that lesson's own
> figures — also matches **KaTeX's radical SVGs**. Their height comes from KaTeX's own `height: inherit`;
> `auto` leaves them with no intrinsic height, and they collapse to zero. The radical is emitted, sits in the
> DOM, and paints nothing.

Nothing except the rendered image could catch it. The DOM was correct. KaTeX emitted `.sqrt` with its SVG.
`.katex-error` count was zero. The hygiene checker confirmed every required KaTeX sequence was present. All
four offline verifiers were green — every model, data, example and hygiene check passing, with 100 % leaf
coverage and both programs byte-identical. (Counts are deliberately not quoted here: the numbers this section
originally carried were the post-repair ones, which is precisely the record-versus-tree drift the review
caught. The current totals are in the final table.)

**Any lesson whose stylesheet contains an unscoped `svg` rule is exposed.** The check is one line: look for a
selector that ends in a bare `svg` under the lesson root, and ask whether KaTeX renders inside that root.

The fix is three parts, and all three are needed:

1. The layout rule is scoped to this lesson's own SVGs by class —
   `.pac-lesson svg:is(.pac-line, .pac-plot, .pac-panel)` — and every SVG the lesson renders carries one of
   them. Tagging the **element** rather than a container keeps the property the original rule was protecting: a
   diagram inside an investigation wrapper still receives the rule, which a `.pac-figure svg` selector would
   have missed.
2. `verify-pac-browser.cjs` measures every `.katex .sqrt svg` on the real page and fails if any paints under

### B1 — the defect class, stated properly

The Phase C record named a repo-wide wrong-mathematics defect class, supplied the one-line check for it,
declared this lesson's instance repaired, and then listed nothing about the class under "Still open". The
reviewer ran that one line and found **24 collapsed radicals live in the Rademacher lesson**, two topics later
in the same uncommitted change set, from the identical root-scoped rule. That is the correct criticism: a
reader of the record would have concluded the class was handled when it had only been handled at this lesson's
boundary.

**The class, as it actually stands.** A bare descendant `svg` selector is unsafe under any root where KaTeX
renders. KaTeX's radical SVGs take their height from `height: inherit`; a rule setting `height: auto` on them
leaves no intrinsic height and they collapse to nothing, so `√(x)` renders as `x`. For a bound, that is the
bound squared.

**The check**, which is worth running before trusting any lesson stylesheet:

```
grep -nE '^\s*\.[a-z-]+(lesson|lab)[a-z-]* svg\s*\{' src/learn/components/lesson-labs/*.css
```

then, for each root it returns, ask whether that lesson's body contains a `\sqrt`, `\widehat`, `\underbrace`
or any other construct KaTeX draws with an SVG.

**The tree-wide result, verified by the reviewer and scoped by the increment owner.** Eight lesson stylesheets
carry the exposed pattern, including the shared `lessons.css`. Of those, only Rademacher rendered radicals —
25 `\sqrt`, 18 `\widehat`, 3 `\underbrace`, with all 24 measured radicals collapsed — and Calibration had one
`\widehat` drawn at 9.078 px against KaTeX's intended 4.641 px. **All three lessons are now fixed**: this one
by class-scoping to `svg:is(.pac-line, .pac-plot, .pac-panel)`, Rademacher by scoping to `svg.rad-drawing`,
Calibration by scoping and measuring. The remaining roots are latent, not live: they will break the moment a
radical is added to those bodies.

That check earned its keep the moment someone ran it against a neighbour. It belongs in the next author's
routine, not in this record as a closed item.
