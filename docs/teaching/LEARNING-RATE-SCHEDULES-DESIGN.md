## Primary research actually read

- [PyTorch2.14 OneCycleLR](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.14/generated/torch.optim.lr_scheduler.OneCycleLR.html): parameter/phase/default/momentum/step-order/state sections inspected. Default two-phase differs from the original paper; minimum depends on both division factors. The title's API needs actual execution, not only an original lookalike.
- [CosineAnnealingLR](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.14/generated/torch.optim.lr_scheduler.CosineAnnealingLR.html): recurrence/closed form, monotonically advancing scheduler call counter and no warm restarts. Its example uses epochs; that does not mandate every use to have an epoch clock. Explain endpoint indexing of used rates.
- [ReduceLROnPlateau](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.14/generated/torch.optim.lr_scheduler.ReduceLROnPlateau.html) and [tagged original source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pytorch/pytorch/v2.14.0/torch/optim/lr_scheduler.py): relevant constructor, step, cooldown, strict comparison and phase interpolation inspected. The prose patience example says an unchanged metric is not adjusted, but the source's strict comparison can count equality as bad. Teach/test the explicit actual predicate and version; do not propagate the conflicting shortcut.
- [SGDR](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1608.03983), selected introduction and section3: cosine phase, optional growing periods and reuse of parameters. The dated empirical claims and speculative broad explanations are not universal guarantees. Section 4.4 was subsequently read: its EEG experiment concerns recorded actual left/right hand and foot movements, not motor imagery. The lesson uses this as a bounded anytime/snapshot example, with no clinical or universal performance claim.
- [Goyal et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.02677), sections2.1–2.2 and selected warmup/large-batch experiment results: local approximation behind scaling, five-epoch gradual ramp in that specific ResNet/ImageNet setup, and failure beyond a regime. Do not treat its numeric defaults as today's universal recipe.
- [Smith cyclical rates](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.01186), sections3.1–3.3: triangular cycles and original linear range test. This is a probe of a changing model, not an independent rate sweep with a guaranteed safe maximum. Do not copy the paper's universal-sounding tuning claims.
- [Smith/Topin super-convergence](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.07120), selected section3: original one-cycle-plus-low-rate-tail and balancing other regularizers. Not all theoretical or empirical sections were reviewed; universal claims and speculative Hessian shortcuts are not adopted.
- [fast.ai lesson18](https://course.fast.ai/Lessons/lesson18.html): official written description/concept/resource list inspected, including cosine/OneCycle and parameter groups. The official embed resolves to https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nlVOG2Nzc3k; that direct lecture and its written page are annotated in the lesson. Playback could not be fetched and was not reviewed; no invented timestamps.
- [PyTorch AMP examples](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.14/notes/amp_examples.html), targeted gradient-accumulation/scaling/skip sections read. The actual CPU GradScaler fixture verifies the selected event boundary; it is not a GPU mixed-precision benchmark.

Additional inspected primary references: [WarmRestarts](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.14/generated/torch.optim.lr_scheduler.CosineAnnealingWarmRestarts.html), constructor/formula/state and step examples; [optimizer restoration](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.14/generated/torch.optim.Optimizer.load_state_dict.html), initialization/load order; [randomness](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.14/notes/randomness.html), dedicated generators and cross-environment limits.
