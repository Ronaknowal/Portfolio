## Independent-review numerical correction

After the initial freeze, independent review identified two overflow cases in exported helpers outside the bounded browser controls. `finiteElbo([1,0],[1e-310,1])` formed `q/posterior` before taking its log, producing infinity despite a finite logarithm. It now computes the difference of log probability, log joint and log evidence, and tests exact joint zero separately. This also retains finite KL when a positive normalized posterior rounds to zero, as with joint `[1e-310,1e100]`. `gaussianKl` now subtracts the two determinant logs instead of taking the log of their potentially overflowing ratio. Covariances `diag(1e-100,1e-100)` and `diag(1e100,1e100)` give the independently derived finite KL `200 log(10)−1`.

## What changed and what was preserved

The old published body explained the family/ELBO idea, compared three Gaussians with a two-mode target, and mentioned CAVI, reparameterization, minibatches, amortization and MCMC checks. It did not actually show an optimization run, distribution geometry, factor updates, gradient contributions or independent exercises with solutions. The rewrite retains and deepens all those useful topics rather than replacing them with an unrelated article.

The original full body is archived in `scratch/variational-inference-native-verification/original-lesson.jsx`, SHA256 `68695b35e219bfae9a451c412f9c86c0a1e9d6ee759eab905ca4611d846aff76`. Its original Python was executed before rewriting, producing `.877,.689,.689` for the original three candidates. Those results remain in the new stable log-density/quadrature program, now accompanied by sign probabilities and explicit candidate-versus-global-optimum qualifications.

The completed route has9 conceptual sections,7 complete Python programs,8 independent practice tasks with separate hints/explained solutions, five subject-specific investigations and one static shared-inference diagram. Those counts describe the completed design; they were not targets or quotas. The lesson introduces unknowns versus variational parameters, derives the fixed-joint ELBO and support contract, calculates an exact restricted-family optimum, explains directional uncertainty, derives actual factor updates and both stochastic gradients, executes an optimizer, verifies minibatch scaling, develops a complete amortized posterior/predictive example, and separates family/optimization/numerical error.

Important accuracy improvements include:

- Unknown evidence is a constant for optimizing a fixed target; its absence prevents evaluating the absolute KL, not dropping that constant in an objective/gradient. Exact same-joint ELBOs across families are comparable. Changed constants, models and stochastic estimates require their own accounting.
- Mean-field is a restriction on q, not proof of posterior independence. In the correlated Normal example, the best reverse-KL factorization underestimates marginal and sum variance but overestimates difference variance. It is not correct to claim all uncertainty is always underestimated.
- Exact CAVI improves the current objective under its normalizability/family assumptions. The Gaussian fixture distinguishes shrinking optimization error from its nonzero family gap; general nonconvex/noisy updates are not given a global or per-step monotonicity guarantee.
- The score/pathwise variance comparison is estimator-specific. At a normalized target p=q, the displayed score estimator is identically zero while the chosen analytical-entropy pathwise estimator is noisy. The effect of an unknown joint normalizer and baseline is explicit. Support/derivative interchange and moving-boundary caveats are adjacent to the derivation.
- Minibatch replication applies to data contributions; the global prior/entropy are not independently duplicated. Amortization shares mapping parameters rather than requiring identical posteriors; changed observation noise requires a changed inference rule. Predictive simulation retains latent and observation uncertainty.
