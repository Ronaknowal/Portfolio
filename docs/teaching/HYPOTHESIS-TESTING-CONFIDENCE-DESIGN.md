## Final teaching decisions

The first-pass route has ten sections: the paired question; sampling/coverage/prediction; raw-data t inference; null tails and interval inversion; useful and negligible effects; prospective power; binary intervals and finite coverage; sampling/decision protections; method selection and resampling; reporting and independent practice. Required reasoning stays visible; full alternative method programs, Gaussian projection justification and complete state tables are optional disclosures. Eight closing changed-input exercises have hints, explained solutions and acceptance criteria, with two earlier prediction checkpoints.

Eight investigations serve different mechanisms: repeated40-interval coverage (known and estimated spread); confidence versus prediction widths; t4 null tails and matching confidence sets; practical superiority and TOST; planned known-variance Normal power; Wald/Wilson/exact binomial coverage; independent families versus dependent repeated looks; and all32 sign assignments. Two compact inline figures map matched observations into differences and expose user-level dependence. Counts describe actual learning jobs, not future requirements.

Fourteen complete Python programs retain the original five timings while changing designs or questions explicitly. Python/NumPy/SciPy runtime setup is provided. Distinct complete programs cover the original paired helper, whole-experiment coverage, tails, equivalence, prediction, power, Holm adjustment, exhaustive optional looks, cluster variance, Welch, Wilson, finite sign flipping, every empirical bootstrap resample and finite proportion coverage.

The MLE discovery warranted a full local Bernoulli section, not a title change: derive the Wilson quadratic by score-test inversion, explain Clopper–Pearson tail equations and endpoint cases, then enumerate every count under a chosen true p. The lab's observed interval and coverage averaging are independent controls. It exposes Wilson under-coverage at n10/p.04 rather than implying universal95% coverage.

Saved discoveries for future owners: [sequential inference](topic-notes/a-b-testing-sequential-analysis-at-scale.md) continues from the exact fair-coin optional-stopping counterexample; [BCa bootstrap](topic-notes/bootstrap-confidence-intervals-bca.md) continues from empirical versus population coverage and records concrete legacy-source claims/signs to reassess.
