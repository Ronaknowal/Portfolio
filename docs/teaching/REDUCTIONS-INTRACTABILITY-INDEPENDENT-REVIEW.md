## Corrected statement

The Cook–Levin paragraph said “The at-most-three-clause form is NP-complete.” That phrase incorrectly suggests a bound on the number of clauses. The intended condition, already used correctly by the neighboring definition, construction, code and proof, is **at most three literals per clause**. Replaced only that phrase with “The form with at most three literals per clause is NP-complete.” A fixed number of clauses is a different, tractable restriction: choose one literal from each clause and check whether the selected literals are consistent, giving polynomially many choices when the clause count is fixed. The correction matters even though the surrounding construction was sound.

Focused check: `scratch/intractability-independent-review/wording-check.json` verifies that reversing the one phrase recovers the old frozen body hash, the final JSX parses, the corrected phrase occurs once, and the other six frozen source hashes are unchanged.
