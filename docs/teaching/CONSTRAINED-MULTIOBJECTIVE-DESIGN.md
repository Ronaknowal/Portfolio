All numerical pictures are explicit teaching calculations, not hardware benchmarks. ## Scope and research boundaries

Do not repeat full KKT/Slater/duality or optimizer catalogues. General nonconvex projection is not guaranteed unique; splitting convergence is conditional. No full NSGA-II/evolutionary or stochastic/chance-constrained solver course is promised. Full robust/chance optimization, fairness policy and production Pareto measurement are better taught by their specialized existing owners; local bridges explain the modeling issue without inventing guarantees.

Primary research completed: Boyd/Vandenberghe section4.7 vector optimization/scalarization, chapter11 barrier methods; Boyd et al. ADMM monograph sections3.1–3.3 assumptions, scaled updates and stopping criteria. Full video playback was not performed; historical software/SDP remarks were not used as current claims.

General projection geometry fits all three stages of its current input. Tables retain horizontal keyboard scrolling on narrow devices; unit-conversion copy calls out the hidden columns.
