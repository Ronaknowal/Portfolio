Implemented Algorithm2 consistently: outer pool gains a new subtree only when its internal checks pass; a later whole-tree turn may retain that valid pool. Did not combine Algorithm3's outer transition weighting with Algorithm2's uniform selection. Fixed maximum depth bounds this educational implementation. |

| [Vehtari etal corrected diagnostic paper v5](https://arxiv.org/html/1903.08008v5) | Sections1–4.3, split/rank/folded R-hat, tie handling, bulk versus function-specific/tail efficiency, paired autocorrelation estimation, limitations and examples. Corrected equation14 uses `(rank−3/8)/(S+1/4)`. Independent SciPy rankdata/normal quantiles validate our standard-library implementation. |

Rank bulk-ESS is not silently inserted into raw-function MCSE. |
