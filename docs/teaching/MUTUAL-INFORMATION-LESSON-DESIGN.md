## Required derivations and qualifications

During writing, the InfoNCE population-expectation versus sampled/trained-score distinction warranted a durable [contrastive-learning destination note](topic-notes/contrastive-learning-simclr-moco-infonce-loss.md). Exact destination inventory confirmed it is planned and has no authored body; the proposed finite enumeration belongs there. MI section 9 includes the necessary qualified bridge without claiming that the destination experiment is implemented. Rate-Distortion's current author owns its fixed-distortion globally bounded finite BA calculation; the present IB update has only the stated block-descent guarantee and a counterexample to interpreting stationarity as global optimality.

1. Derive finite MI from expected conditional log-loss improvement and from KL of joint versus product. Define pointwise log ratio separately from its expectation; mean reduction does not imply each observation reduces uncertainty.
2. Establish nonnegativity, symmetry, independence iff zero, finite discrete upper bounds, chain rule and conditional MI. Conditioning can increase or decrease MI; XOR and common-cause examples explain both.
3. Derive DPI using `I(Y;X,Z)` in two orders. State the exact Markov factorization and equality criterion `I(Y;X|Z)=0`; deterministic task-sufficient representations need not preserve every input detail.
4. Define stochastic encoder versus its marginal, alphabet size versus mutual information and deterministic `I(X;Z)=H(Z)` only in the finite setting. Distinguish compact information from bytes of a real saved tensor or a coding theorem. A nuisance independent of Y adds rate without relevance in the explicit product example.
5. For β between0 and1, DPI gives `J≥(1−β)I(X;Z)≥0`, so an input-independent encoder attains0. Larger β prioritizes relevance, not necessarily complete reconstruction; arbitrary nuisance does not become necessary. Compare candidate objective values without presenting a sampled parameter grid as an exact general IB frontier.
6. Derive finite IB via an auxiliary free energy with independent encoder, marginal reference and predictive representatives. Every exact block minimizes that free energy, and after marginal/decoder recomputation it equals J plus the constant βI(X;Y). Nonnegative bounded free energy gives decreasing objective values; it does not prove global optimality, unique parameters or arbitrary optimizer convergence.
7. Derive VIB gap identities and the coefficient conversion: for β>0, minimizing `rateUpper+βCE` is equivalent to `CE+(1/β)rateUpper`. The Alemi paper calls the compression coefficient beta, the reciprocal of this lesson's relevance-priority beta. No unsupported cancellation of H(Y) when changing the target law or scoring different datasets.
8. Define general MI as KL(PXY||PX⊗PY), allowing infinity. Finite differential-entropy differences are not a definition when subtraction is undefined. Separate coordinate units from log units: invertible transforms of each variable preserve MI, but quantization/noise may change it. Deterministic continuous non-atomic output can make MI infinite, while deterministic finite output has at most its discrete entropy.
9. Estimation, prediction, causation, generalization, privacy and fairness answer different questions. Population I(Z;A)=0 is true marginal independence; it does not promise conditional independence given side information, a finite-sample estimate's correctness or every fairness criterion. Avoid the old vague implication that exact independence itself still contains arbitrary marginal proxies.
