## Mathematical decisions and nuanced cases

- General dual definitions retain the original variable domain explicitly. A constant multiplier is optimized separately from the primal variable; treating every constraint as a fixed penalty or minimizing jointly in all variables would change the problem.
- Explain minimization versus infimum and supremum versus attained maximum at first use. Keep finite optimum assumptions explicit before using a gap. Primal infeasibility/unboundedness have extended-real conventions; a single meaningless −∞ bound says little.
- For a differentiable convex problem on all of R^n with convex inequalities and affine equalities, supplied KKT implies global optimality. On a retained convex domain, stationarity uses minimization/normal-cone conditions instead of blindly setting a boundary derivative to zero. The resource example solves its nonnegative local domains directly.
- The no-Slater quadratic constraint is convex yet degenerate. Failure of the simple sufficient Slater test is not proof of positive duality gap or absence of all certificates in every formulation. Constraint representation changes qualification and multiplier behavior.
- Active means zero slack; positive multiplier implies active by complementarity, but active does not imply positive. Zero price means no nonzero first-order supporting price in the unique smooth case, not immunity to all finite/tightening changes. Duplicated constraints can give nonunique multipliers even with a unique primal solution.
- Perturbing the right-hand side has derivative −λ under the declared `g≤u` convention. Multiplying g by a positive constant rescales its multiplier reciprocally. Objective and constraint units determine price units; raw multiplier magnitudes across differently scaled constraints are not comparable.
- An approximate KKT residual alone is not a universal quantitative optimality bound. A justified finite dual value plus a truly feasible primal candidate bounds suboptimality. Floating-point feasibility tolerances and analytical reconstruction must be reported honestly.
- Saddle inequalities are derived under primal/dual attainment and zero gap; exchanging inf/sup without hypotheses can fail. Full conjugate duality, general cone programming and advanced splitting are optional links, not silently claimed coverage.
