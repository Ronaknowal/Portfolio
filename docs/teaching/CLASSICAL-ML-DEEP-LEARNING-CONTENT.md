### Reconciled teaching boundaries

- **Preparation, selection and evaluation:** Scaling/Imputation, Cross-Validation, Regularization, Feature Selection and Bias–Variance supply the metric and data-split definitions needed for their immediate examples. Evaluation Metrics later develops the distinctions fully. Selecting a model or threshold on development observations does not turn those observations into an independent performance estimate. Selection, permutation reliance, SHAP attribution and physical causality answer different questions.
- **Statistical criteria:** Regularization preserves AIC, BIC and MDL with their assumptions and worked calculations; Bias–Variance links to that treatment while explaining optimism and learning curves. A sufficient learning bound is not a measured minimum sample requirement. Dataset-dependent outcomes, including weak or unsuccessful methods, remain visible.
- **Graphs and uncertainty:** Bayesian Networks connects local conditional information to Feature Selection, supplies d-separation and the distinction between observation and intervention, and leads to CRF's conditional sequence model. Conditional independence implied by a graph and numerical dependence under particular parameters are distinguished. The causal section retains identification, estimation and individual counterfactuals as separate tasks.
- **Limited labels:** Semi-Supervised Learning distinguishes using unlabeled inputs from acquiring new labels; Active Learning accounts for initial, development and queried labels. Their real experiments retain cases where the baseline or a simpler policy wins. Calibration and coverage must not be inferred merely from confident scores or useful acquisition rankings.
- **Rare events and uncertainty:** Imbalanced Learning distinguishes class sampling, weighted score meaning, action costs and top-k capacity; later Metrics and Calibration own wider evaluation and probability/set interpretation. Its Yeast study removed 22 verified exact duplicate protein IDs before retaining the corrected fixed-procedure comparison; original and analyzed denominators remain explicit. Calibration separates class probabilities from top confidence, probability-map fitting from conformal calibration, and marginal coverage from subgroup/selected-case guarantees. Exact ranks, ties and finite-precision score equality have concrete fixtures.
- **Automated experiments:** AutoML separates the conditional search space, proposal strategy and performance estimator. Its local neural-network bridge makes the architecture discussion readable before deep learning. Fidelity pruning, expected improvement, operation mixtures and hard deployment constraints each use a different representation; the real finite search is labeled as a replay, not an unperformed optimizer benchmark. Dedicated NAS and meta-learning retain their specialist ownership.
- **Generalization guarantees:** PAC/VC introduces distributional assumptions and class-wide guarantees; Rademacher Complexity distinguishes a fixed sample's predictor class from the loss class, exact inner optimization from simulation estimates, and an upper bound from a forecast of test error. The real bounded-norm example preserves strong observed accuracy alongside vacuous bounds and explicitly states its fixed-corpus limitations. These distinctions lead into problem formulation rather than implying that a theorem repairs data leakage.
- **Sequence inference:** HMM separates filtering, smoothing, forecasting, marginal decisions and the most likely legal whole path. Its changed-future experiment leaves past filtering unchanged while revising smoothing. Independent recording boundaries determine expected counts; supervised real tagging is distinguished from unlabeled-state EM. The shared EWT extract supports continuity into CRF without claiming that HMM's small development improvement establishes an algorithm ranking.
- **Problem formulation and forecasting:** The late foundational titles consolidate ideas introduced locally earlier in the module. Prediction-time availability, event versus arrival time, revised records, repeated entities and delayed outcomes determine permissible data. Time-series diagrams distinguish issuance time, feature availability and target availability; a reporting delay changes historical feature construction as well as eligible training labels. The supervised capstone carries these decisions into an explicit repair and untouched-evaluation workflow.
- **Beginning deep learning:** Perceptrons introduces forward computation and activation geometry; Backpropagation derives gradients and automatic differentiation; Loss Functions explains the objective being differentiated. Normalization separates reduction axes, learned affine parameters and running state. Transfer Learning includes a local description of residual adapters before the dedicated residual lesson, and distinguishes actual reuse of learned weights from initialization or hyperparameter transfer. Initialization treats average signal scale and directional gradient behavior separately, with a precisely delimited μP implementation.
- **Residual paths and masking:** Residual Connections preserves direct-plus-correction geometry while demonstrating cancellation, projection, normalization and zero-initialization distinctions. Dropout differentiates element/channel/example/batch masks, expectation from nonlinear averaging, module mode from gradient recording, and expected active branches from actual branch calls. Actual digit results retain cases where no masking performs better; MC disagreement is not renamed calibrated uncertainty.
- **Spatial operators:** Convolution teaches patch correspondence, channel mixing, shared-gradient accumulation, asymmetric alignment, pooling loss, coordinate ancestry and exact holes before its real classifier. Theoretical receptive-field span differs from observed pixels and local gradient influence. Transpose is an adjoint, not an inverse; parameter/MAC ratios are not latency. Saved specimen views show actual post-ReLU maps and probabilities, since raw preactivations/logits were not retained. The next architecture lesson remains in sequence.
