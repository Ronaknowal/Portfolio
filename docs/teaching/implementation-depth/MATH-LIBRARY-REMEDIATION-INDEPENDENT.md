## Findings and resolution

**R1 — optimizer-family library coverage.** The title and core mechanism teach AdaGrad, RMSProp and Adam in addition to momentum SGD/AdamW. A parity fixture for only the latter two did not yet give the learner the ordinary API route/settings for the three other built-in families. Requested a narrow extension of the supplied-gradient program and mapping table, with parameter/state comparisons and the existing skipped/zero/resume cases. Also requested an explicit boundary for LARS/LAMB: their custom block conventions are retained; PyTorch's built-in optimizer list does not supply those named algorithms, and a tensor/custom or external implementation needs convention matching. No universal provider-equivalence or artificial dependency is required.

**Resolved.** The author added AdaGrad, RMSProp and coupled Adam to the actual download, mapping table, interpreted output and state checks. AdaGrad's accumulator, learning-rate decay and overridden epsilon; RMSProp's alpha, noncentered/no-momentum mode; and Adam's explicit coupled-decay flag are all declared. The updated native receipt passes all five methods, and the final body explains why zero-with-decay can move AdaGrad/RMSProp while zero-without-decay leaves their parameters fixed but changes RMSProp history. It explicitly distinguishes custom LARS/LAMB variants from maintained built-ins. Independent full weighted-history checks and the final structure/source pass confirm the repair. No further training experiment or package dependency was needed.
