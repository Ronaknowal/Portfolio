A learner should be able to diagnose changed support points, duplicate features, a bad kernel, a stalled pair update, an uncalibrated score, preprocessing leakage and inappropriate resource assumptions. The page must work as an ordinary reading sequence before anyone touches a control.

Derive the necessary quadratic minimization and constrained lower bound on the page; **Convex Duality & Lagrangian Methods (KKT Conditions)** is a useful deeper proof review, not an unexplained requirement for the first picture. **Functional Analysis & RKHS** owns the full representation theorem; the finite Gram argument and concrete polynomial feature map are proved here. Preserve actual module navigation: previous GBDT, next Naive Bayes; do not choose a different published successor.

## Reading and derivation sequence

1. **Choose among correct boundaries.** A two-class sensor example has observations, features, labels and future decisions. Explain `f(x)=w·x+b`, sign, normal and projection; derive the distance by subtracting `f(x)w/||w||²`. Positive rescaling preserves sign and geometric distance but changes raw score. Derive canonical `y f≥1` from a strictly separating score and show minimizing half squared norm maximizes the canonical corridor. Explicitly reject w=0 as a geometric hyperplane.
2. **Which observations hold the boundary?** Compute the one-dimensional fixture `[-2,-1]` negative and `[1,z]` positive. With `r=min(1,z)`, `w=2/(1+r)`, `b=(1-r)/(1+r)`. Compare moving z from 2 to 3 with moving it to .5. Local certificate reasoning explains why the second move changes the optimum. Relate linear prediction stability to `|w·δ|≤||w||||δ||`; do not convert this training geometry into a generalization guarantee without distribution/radius/capacity assumptions.
3. **Pay for margin shortfalls.** Derive minimal slack `max(0,1-yf)`, distinguish correct-but-inside from misclassified, and compare hinge to logistic loss in the prior lesson. Work the symmetric two-point objective completely. For x=±1, y=±1, alpha=min(C,.5), w=2alpha; at C<.5 an interval of optimal b exists. A conflicting-label duplicate fixture has w=0, alpha=C and b∈[-1,1], so no distance division or false hard-margin limit. Derive the sum-versus-average convention `lambda=1/(Cn)` and the effect of duplicated rows or per-row penalties.
4. **Build a certificate rather than trust a solver.** Introduce nonnegative multipliers as lower-bound weights, expand the Lagrangian, minimize separately in w,b,slack and derive the dual with equality/box constraints. Slater holds for finite C>0 by choosing large positive slack, so an attained feasible primal/dual equality certifies optimality. Show weak duality directly and display primal, dual and gap. Derive all three KKT rows including equality cases; recover b from a free coefficient or an admissible interval if no free coefficient exists. Prove unique w from strict convexity in w plus convex feasible averaging, and show why that proof says nothing about unique b/alpha. A duplicate-positive example redistributes the same total coefficient without changing the function.
5. **Change similarity without changing the finite dual.** Expand `(x·z)²` into `(x1²,sqrt(2)x1x2,x2²)` and work the XOR fixture explicitly. Show a two-dimensional projection of the lifted coordinates with the separating plane's relevant coordinate; do not masquerade a flat cartoon as the complete 3D geometry. Derive Gram PSD by squared norm, symmetry and closure for nonnegative combinations/products. State polynomial parameter restrictions; illustrate an invalid symmetric two-point matrix with a negative quadratic-form witness. Explain RBF bandwidth, local score contributions and finite-support far-field limit b. Include a short proof route through the exponential series and its positive features, with Hilbert-space details linked to RKHS.
6. **Make one legal optimization move.** Let delta change alpha_j while alpha_i changes by `-y_i y_j delta`; intersect both box intervals. Along that line the dual improvement is `g delta - q delta²/2`, q=`Kii+Kjj−2Kij`≥0. Derive the derivative, clip the optimum, and handle q=0 by endpoint comparison. Use the same derivation for opposite-label diagonals and same-label anti-diagonals. Implement a deterministic bounded pair-coordinate educational solver, report whether its primal-dual criterion passed, and disclose its full-Gram/work cost. Never use a random inactivity counter as a mathematical convergence certificate.
7. **Select a model on data it did not fit.** Preserve the old 200-row noisy-moons generator but reserve train/validation/test with recorded seeds/indices. Fit scaling on training rows, fit a bounded C/gamma grid, and compute exact model scores for plots. A single-metric validation matrix selects a stored model; companion training accuracy and support count remain distinct quantities. The browser includes no hidden test-tuning loop. Complete Python uses internal CV and an untouched test assessment; print baseline, selected parameters, objective/output shapes and inspected support counts. Do not compare machine timings or claim dataset-independent winners.
8. **Use library contracts deliberately.** Binary sign/classes and multiclass one-vs-one training versus decision-function presentation; class/sample C_i; sparse scaling and missing/categorical preparation; SVC, LinearSVC and SGD objective/intercept differences. Explicit calibration includes the entire pipeline within each fold, and its fitted probability is evaluated separately from the base decision score. Out-of-fold calibration is not universally unbiased after hyperparameter selection; keep a held-out assessment and note nested tuning when needed. Current SVC API deprecation is attached to the recipe/version.
9. **Replace the classification margin with a regression tolerance.** Derive epsilon-insensitive absolute residual loss, the two slack inequalities and the dual signed coefficients `alpha−alpha*`, box and zero-sum constraint. Show why interior-tube observations can have zero coefficients while equality is degenerate. Work symmetric targets ±a at x=±1 (center 0), choosing the symmetry-valid b=0 optimum `w=min(2C,max(a−epsilon,0))`. Plot vertical target-unit error, tube and excess—not perpendicular classification distance. Derive target scaling y'=y/s giving epsilon'=epsilon/s and C'=C/s for equivalent fits. Execute the old regression dataset in a training-fitted feature/target transform with held-out MAE/RMSE and a mean baseline.
10. **Use meaningful features and bound costs.** A concrete two-mer count kernel gives a sequence-based application: compute overlapping counts and their dot product, explain what order/position information it loses, and verify training-versus-query Gram shape. It is a synthetic mechanism example, not evidence of clinical or protein performance. Explain full Gram bytes separately from cache memory, kernel inference O(nSV·kernel-cost), linear weight collapse and feature dimension/nnz. Derive the RBF random-feature expectation from cosine plus Gaussian characteristic function, and Nyström factorization with eigenvalue threshold assumptions; include complete fitted-training-only approximation comparisons without guaranteed rankings. Link deeper approximation and one-class novelty work to their existing owners after exact inventory resolution.
11. **Diagnose and transfer.** Independent changed tasks have optional hints before explained solutions, including a small end-to-end report. Preserve module next-step order and give targeted written/video alternatives.

Combine closely related calculations when it improves readability, not to reach a count. Every visible block has a prediction question, imports, data, execution command before the first optional program, exact output from pinned runtime and an interpreted result. Displayed helper functions must receive changed-input checks, not only pasted fixture assertions.

Changed practice includes: rescale a score without moving its boundary; move the positive support inward; redistribute duplicate coefficients while preserving w; diagnose all-SV data at high and low C; derive C after duplicating rows; find a negative Gram witness; compute a two-variable clipped update including q=0; fix calibration leakage; distinguish OVO model count from output shape; change regression target units; construct two strings sharing counts but losing positions; price approximation feature construction separately from classifier epochs. The capstone preserves split identities and compares a baseline, model selection, final holdout and error examples without claiming universal best performance. Hints and explanations are separate disclosures. No LeetCode quota applies to this ML topic.
