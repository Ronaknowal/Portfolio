## Visual / interaction choices

`DegreeNormalizedAgreementFigure` compares two constructed score columns on a three-node chain. White is raw score; amber is degree-normalized score. Both panels use the same explicit linear scale; captions distinguish scores from probabilities and zero graph penalty from the full fitted optimum.

`ConsistencyTargetLab` has three real range controls, immediate mask/loss/logit-gradient outputs and reset. The weak view is restricted to class-1-majority outputs to isolate one transition. The explanation states that supplied outputs are controlled, no images are augmented and no network is fitted. No prediction gate. It complements the existing three labs instead of replacing them with a generic widget. Scoped charcoal/amber styling, labels, visible focus, responsive panels and text equivalents are present; rendered keyboard/mobile checks await root.
