## Scope and completed teaching

The [individual design](BACKTRACKING-DIVIDE-LESSON-DESIGN.md), [lesson](../../src/learn/data/topics/backtracking-divide-and-conquer.jsx) and [blueprint](../../src/learn/data/curriculum/blueprints/backtracking-divide-and-conquer.js) cover both halves of the title. Complexity & Recursion supplies frames, termination and cost models; Binary Search, Sorting & Two-Pointer Patterns supplies ordered merging. This lesson builds on those prerequisites rather than treating generic recursion and merge sort as new material.

- Candidate-choice trees versus decomposition of one input; the useful distinction and binary-search taxonomy nuance.
- Subsets with explicit occurrence identity, a shared path, copied outputs and exact choose/explore/undo ownership. Soundness, completeness, termination and restoration arguments accompany the code; output costs are separated from auxiliary state.
- Positive target subsets with overshoot and remaining-sum bounds, plus the reason signed values require different bounds. Same-input pruned/unpruned traces preserve the answer set. Frequency-based unique permutations and positive reusable combinations have their own identity, canonical-order and termination contracts.
- N-Queens column/diagonal geometry; word-search path-local used cells; input preservation and cleanup even after an early successful return. Decision, counting, enumeration and optimization stopping rules differ. Feasibility pruning, branch-and-bound and memoization are distinguished.
- Strict inversion counting via sorted cross-boundary pairs, including equal-value handling and ranking/adjacent-swap applications.
- Nonempty maximum-subarray summaries: total, prefix, suffix and best. The split cases justify each field, the richer return contract reduces combine work, and ordered chunk processing/segment trees provide a practical connection. All-negative values, witness ties, arithmetic/bit costs and diagnostic storage are explicit.
- Four independent exercises with hints and explained solutions: balanced parentheses, interval witnesses, ranking disagreements and a concrete incomplete-memoization-key counterexample. Their acceptance cases and proofs supplement external practice.

Scope/title review found no need to rename: the completed mechanisms fit the existing title. Bitmask subset-state encoding was routed to the existing [dynamic-programming destination note](topic-notes/dynamic-programming-states-transitions-optimization.md), with prerequisites and reasons. This is a partial routing of the existing broader bit-manipulation concern, not a claim that XOR tricks, fixed-width conventions and all bitwise interview coverage now have an implemented owner. No new topic or global catalogue audit was undertaken.
