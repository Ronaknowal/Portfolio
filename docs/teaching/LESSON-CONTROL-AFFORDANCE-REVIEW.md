## Changes and teaching contract

- **Evaluation Metrics:** forecast diamonds are real controls. Drag or use the keyboard to edit A and B independently while observed circles remain fixed. Residual lengths, proportional squared areas, MAE, RMSE, SSE and R² follow the same input state. The inline example keeps a fixed ruler and area scale; the extended residual lab freezes its domain during a gesture and synchronizes its numeric editors. Reset restores the original example; matching observations supplies a zero-error null. Read-only probability rulers use ticks and identify the actual item selector. Retired learner-answer helpers and instructions were removed.
- **Calibration & Conformal Prediction:** the inline conditioning figure now has four live forecast/rate sliders, .01 steps, fixed equal population shares, reset, contrasting presets and a calibrated null. Both diagrams and their captions/table describe the current state. Equal class-1 forecasts and equal confidences are pooled under their respective conditioning variables. Conditional calibration is checked at every point, rather than inferred from agreement of overall averages. Complementary decimal confidences pool despite floating-point roundoff; .5 selects class 1; 0 and 1 remain valid inputs.
- **Shared controls:** dark/amber defaults cover controls outside lab wrappers while topic styles retain their own encodings. Selected, disabled and keyboard-focus states remain distinct. Defaults also cover previously unstyled selects/text fields and native range accent/hit areas. Static diagrams are not all converted into labs: marks that are read-only must look and read as annotations.

There are no learner-prediction fields, optional guesses or reveal gates. Editing a model forecast is subject matter, not asking a learner to predict an answer. Measured experiments, datasets, scientific boundaries and frozen test reports retain their original roles.
