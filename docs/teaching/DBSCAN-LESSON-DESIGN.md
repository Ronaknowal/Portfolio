## Exact fixtures and elementary author calculations

Main street rows A–J: `[-1.75,-1.5,-1.25,-1,1,1.25,1.5,1.75,0,4]`, y=0. Physical distance units are invented meters. At ε=1,m=4 the count vector is `[4,4,4,5,5,4,4,4,3,1]`; A–H core, I shared border, J noise. At ε=.75 I is noise; at 1.25 I becomes core and merges the two components. All critical coordinates/radii are exactly representable dyadic numbers.

Variable-density counterexample: x=`[0,.125,.25,.375,.75,.875,1,1.125,5,5.75,6.5,7.25]`, m=3. Joining the diffuse last four needs ε≥.75, while the first two dense groups already join at ε=.375. No single radius realizes the intended three groups. Replacing the last four by `[5,5.125,5.25,5.375]` makes ε=.25 recover all three: the intended null.

[author-calculations.py](drafts/dbscan-density-based-clustering/author-calculations.py) and its [compact JSON](drafts/dbscan-density-based-clustering/author-calculations.json) record the actual stage-1/2 probes: five street radii, reversal, three density-conflict radii and null, duplicates/m=1, OPTICS extraction, selected mutual edges, HDBSCAN and seven Iris parameter/representation cases. NumPy 2.3.5/SciPy 1.18.1/sklearn 1.9.1. Initial JSON serialization failed on a legitimate undefined OPTICS distance; changed its storage to null and reran. Explicit `copy=True` fixes the chosen HDBSCAN setting rather than relying on its changing future default. This is author arithmetic, not formal independent or comprehensive native verification.

The useful Iris contrast is empirical on these 150 rows: standardized ε=.5,m=5 retains 116; m=10 retains 61 and has assigned-row ARI 1.0. The full-row score/coverage exposes why a perfect conditional score is insufficient. No parameter is selected by species. All printed full-program outputs still require exact program execution in phase two, even where their constituent values were probed now.
