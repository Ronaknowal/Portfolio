# Authoring notes: Dimensionality Reduction & Manifold Analysis for Neural Data

Canonical topic ID: `dimensionality-reduction-manifold-analysis-for-neural-data`.

## 2026-09-12 — Match the reconstruction target to the scientific question

- Status: open
- Origin: [PCA content-first design](../PCA-LESSON-DESIGN.md), [manuscript §§6,10.3,10.5](../drafts/pca-dimensionality-reduction/lesson.md).
- Ownership: this neural topic already plans held-out reconstruction and embedding stability (`src/learn/data/curriculum/neural-expansion.js`, its individual blueprint). It is the right home for neural measurement/noise, trial/session splits, temporal dependence and the distinction between population coordinates and dynamics. The general PCA lesson supplies a short bridge rather than prescribing biological preprocessing.
- Existing coverage inspected: blueprint outcome/sequence includes population matrices, PCA, nonlinear hypotheses, time/behavior, held-out error and stability. No full new neural lesson was written or audited in this task.
- Proposal: distinguish error against held-out **noisy input**, error against an independent/clean target where justified, and downstream behavioral/decoding value. For a single fixed orthonormal PCA basis with nested component sets, squared input reconstruction error decreases for every row as k grows, including held-out rows. Minimizing it alone selects all available dimensions; use an explicit compression budget/complexity cost, an appropriate probabilistic evaluation or a scientifically justified target. Do not promise a U-shaped held-out input-reconstruction curve.
- Worked bridge: PCA's general lesson uses a mean-only baseline and a chosen error budget on a 133/45 Wine split; it separately contrasts signal(s,s) with noise(e,−e), which projection removes, versus noise(e,e), which it preserves. Adapt the mechanism to a neural setting with realistic measurement and noise assumptions; the wine numbers are not neural evidence.
- Additional connection: specify whether rows are time bins, trials or conditions and columns neurons/features. Ordinary PCA is unchanged by permuting rows; a trajectory drawn through scores in time order adds temporal information that the covariance fit did not learn. Compare with the separate Neural Population Dynamics & Latent-State Models owner when dynamics become the outcome.
- Prerequisites/boundaries: refresh orthogonal projection/error decomposition and fit/transform. Choose validation splits by the actual participant/session/trial claim; avoid treating correlated time bins as independent held-out biological replicates. A stable or predictive latent coordinate is not automatically a named cognitive variable.
- Evidence: exact projection/Pythagorean identity derived in the PCA manuscript; [Jolliffe & Cadima2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4792409/), standard PCA definition and sample/interpretation issues, read12 September2026; [scikit-learn leakage guide](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html#data-leakage), same date. A future neural author must research the neural dataset, measurement assumptions and validation protocol; none was empirically established here.
- Resolution: not yet assessed by the neural author. Reassess/adapt this proposal during that authorized topic; the complete general PCA content draft is available for reuse without pretending its visuals have been implemented.
