## 2026-09-10 — Separate empirical resampling from population coverage

- Status: open
- Origin: [Hypothesis Testing & Confidence Intervals design](../HYPOTHESIS-TESTING-CONFIDENCE-DESIGN.md), section9 and its complete exact empirical-bootstrap program.
- Destination and ownership rationale: the exact inventory command resolves this planned Statistics for LLM Evaluation topic. A legacy source file exists at `src/learn/data/topics/bootstrap-confidence-intervals-bca.jsx`, but file existence is not publication or review. This topic is the right home for BCa mechanics, assumptions and evaluation-specific resampling units; the foundation lesson supplies the empirical-versus-population distinction.
- Existing coverage inspected: the first 120 lines of that source, including the introduction, core intuition and bias-correction definition. This was a scoped read, not verification of the entire legacy lesson.
- Concrete issues to reassess: the introduction claims every listed metric lacks analytic standard errors, although a pairwise independent Bernoulli win rate has a binomial sampling model. It asserts uncited universal-looking coverage percentages and method adoption; these need primary evidence with datasets/conditions or removal. The bias-correction prose says a proportion below .5 gives positive z0, while its own z0=Φ⁻¹(proportion) formula gives a negative value. That sign is a direct mathematical contradiction. Do not carry these statements forward as established facts.
- Idea and learning benefit: exact enumeration of all empirical resamples eliminates Monte Carlo uncertainty about that empirical distribution; it does not establish nominal repeated-population coverage. For the origin's five differences [2,4,−1,3,2], all 5^5 ordered resamples produce a percentile interval [.4,3.2], unlike the exact-Normal-model t interval [−.322941,4.322941]. Explain each assumption and approximation before comparing decisions.
- Proposed treatment: derive percentile/basic/BCa from the statistic and resampling unit, with signs and transformations checked by changed examples. State the regularity regime behind any higher-order accuracy statement. Include boundary/degenerate data and an example where the ordinary jackknife acceleration denominator is zero; an all-zero Bernoulli sample cannot reveal unseen successes by resampling itself.
- Prerequisites and boundaries: distinguish finite bootstrap-replicate variability, empirical-distribution approximation, coverage under a specified data-generating model, and dependence/cluster sampling. Symmetry alone is not a universal finite-sample coverage certificate. Resample whole independent matched units for paired comparisons.
- Evidence: [SciPy bootstrap reference](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.bootstrap.html), read September10,2026, parameter `paired` and Notes/Warns on degenerate BCa distribution/NaN endpoints; current displayed manual1.18.0. The origin's finite enumeration and changed-data tests are recorded in [its verification](../HYPOTHESIS-TESTING-CONFIDENCE-VERIFICATION.md). Research primary BCa theorems and exact conditions during receiving authoring; this note does not certify uncited legacy adoption/coverage figures.
