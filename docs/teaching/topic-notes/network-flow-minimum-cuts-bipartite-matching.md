## 2026-09-10 — Turn a matching lower bound into an exact cover under bipartite structure

- Status: implemented in the destination; author verification recorded below, integrated review remains separate.
- Origin: `reductions-p-np-computational-intractability`, [design](../REDUCTIONS-INTRACTABILITY-LESSON-DESIGN.md), section 7's vertex-cover contract and matching lower-bound investigation.
- Destination rationale: the current destination plan already owns bipartite matching, augmenting paths and equal primal/cut certificates. This is the natural next place to explain why a special graph structure supports stronger results than the origin's arbitrary-graph 2-approximation.
- Existing coverage: origin teaches maximal (unextendable) versus maximum matching, a disjoint matching lower bound on cover size, exact bounded cover search and arbitrary-graph NP completeness. Destination is planned; no claim that the equality or cover reconstruction is already taught there.
- Proposed treatment: after maximum bipartite matching, derive the alternating-reachability minimum vertex cover: start at unmatched left vertices, follow unmatched edges left-to-right and matched edges right-to-left; return unvisited left vertices plus visited right vertices. Prove every edge is covered and the size equals the maximum matching. Contrast a triangle, where bipartite structure fails and maximum matching size 1 differs from minimum cover size 2. Use actual matching edges, residual/alternating directions, reachable sets and returned cover, with independent tiny subset oracles.
- Learning benefit: learners connect an arbitrary feasible matching lower bound, a maximum matching, a cut and an exact cover; they do not confuse maximal with maximum or transfer a bipartite theorem to all graphs.
- Prerequisites/boundaries: bipartition, augmenting paths, maximum matching optimality; handle isolated vertices, disconnected components, empty matchings and return original vertex identities. Full proof/implementation belongs in destination, not as an unexplained aside in complexity theory.
- Evidence to review: MIT combinatorial optimization matching/duality course notes https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-433-combinatorial-optimization-fall-2003/pages/lecture-notes/; direct course page inspected. Receiving author must inspect the relevant full primary theorem source and verify its implementation. The origin's independently tested arbitrary-graph matching lower bound does not establish this stronger equality.
- Resolution: included in section5 of `src/learn/data/topics/network-flow-minimum-cuts-bipartite-matching.jsx`, native `networkFlowExamples.matching`, `MatchingCoverLab` and `solveMatching`. The complete cover/Hall proof, alternating directions, original identities, empty/unequal partitions and triangle limit are taught.
