- Proposed treatment: begin the symmetry portion with the origin's exact four-channel square calculation, then change one assumption at a time: arbitrary graph relabeling, geometric vector channels, continuous rotations, finite grids/resampling and boundaries. Derive the actual architectural condition before discussing learned performance. Retain accessible special cases and distinguish exact from approximate equivariance.
- Concrete examples: a cyclic shift commutes with square rotations but fails a reflection. Applying a scalar nonlinearity identically to every channel commutes with channel permutation, but coordinatewise ReLU need not commute with a geometric rotation/sign-reflection matrix. On a finite square, eight conjugates can be averaged exactly; a continuous group is not obtained by asserting that eight samples cover every angle. A physically position-dependent sensor calibration may make weight tying the wrong modeling assumption.
- Practice: specify the output type and its transformation, calculate both routes on a changed input, then prove the whole-map contract or exhibit a counterexample. For a learned model, independently evaluate prediction quality with controlled splits/ablations; a zero algebraic equivariance defect alone does not establish accuracy or generalization.
- Prerequisites and boundaries: the origin now explains left actions, representation matrices, tied weights, finite averaging and exact counterexamples with local proofs and complete programs. This destination must introduce any continuous-group measure, irreducible-feature, geometric tensor, architecture, sampling or statistical prerequisites it actually uses. Review existing broad performance and physical-transformation assertions against their precise primary sources instead of carrying them forward automatically.
- Evidence: Cohen and Welling's [original G-CNN paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/cohenc16.pdf), particularly the two-representation condition and finite implementation context, inspected 11 September 2026; source reading and exact SymPy/Fraction design calculations in the origin design. The sensor example is an explanatory mathematical model, not an empirical sensor benchmark.
