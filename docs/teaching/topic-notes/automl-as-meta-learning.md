## 2026-09-12 — Teach transfer between tasks with task-level evidence

- Status: open
- Origin: `automl-neural-architecture-search-nas`, [prepared foundation and canonical coverage record](../drafts/automl-neural-architecture-search-nas/design.md), especially manuscript section 6.
- Destination and ownership rationale: this existing Meta-Learning topic is the natural owner of cross-task portfolios, warm starts and learned recommendations. Row-level search and the introductory neural architecture mechanism remain in the earlier classical lesson.
- Existing coverage: the originating packet distinguishes search, greedy ensemble selection, stacking and transfer. Its constructed portfolio has old-task losses A=(0.10,0.50), B=(0.50,0.10), C=(0.25,0.25): C is the best single default, while A/B together cover the old tasks better. New-task losses (0.40,0.40,0.20) reverse that portfolio advantage. The destination's identity/module was verified; its body has not been audited here. Review its current content before choosing additions.
- Idea and learning benefit: distinguish learning from more rows of one task from learning which procedure will work on a new task. The learner should explain why task-level separation is needed and recognize negative transfer.
- Proposed treatment: introduce the task/configuration performance matrix and task-level train/validation/test roles. Develop complementary portfolio construction, relative landmarks or justified task features, warm starts, ranking and performance prediction where they improve the topic. Include an observed transfer comparison with a simple default and a meaningful negative-transfer control. Investigate current learned portfolios and pretrained tabular models with explicit pretraining/task overlap, licensing and resource assumptions; do not import a permanent library ranking.
- Explanation/example: let a learner choose a small portfolio on old tasks, predict a new task's choice, then expose a changed task where its preferred configurations fail. Separate task observations used to fit a recommender from observations used to select a configuration within the new task. Compare against the best single old-task default; show actual costs and uncertainty only where measured.
- Prerequisites and boundaries: use the earlier AutoML search-space and validation definitions as a bridge. Row-level CV within known tasks does not establish transfer to unseen tasks. Similar task summaries do not guarantee identical rankings. Ensemble weights from greedy selection are not automatically Bayesian posterior model probabilities. Specialized representation/few-shot learning may belong to neighboring topics after a reasoned scope decision.
