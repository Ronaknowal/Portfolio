## 2026-09-13 — Preserve target mass when averaging gradients across unequal ranks

- Status: open
- Origin: [Mini-Batches, Training Loops & Gradient Accumulation](../drafts/mini-batches-training-loops-gradient-accumulation/design.md), manuscript §§5 and 8.
- Destination and ownership rationale: this topic's brief already plans single-process equivalence, normalization, synchronization timing and uneven inputs. DDP is the stronger home for an actual multi-process reproduction, including no-sync and rank participation; the originating lesson supplies the scalar prerequisite bridge.
- Idea and learning benefit: distinguish average-of-rank-means from a global target-weighted objective. Eligible token counts or class-weight sums can differ even when rectangular input batch shapes match. Learners should predict and repair an incorrect gradient without changing the learning rate to conceal it.
- Existing coverage: **planned**, checked `src/learn/data/curriculum/gpu-expansion.js` Data Parallelism (DDP) plan on 13 September 2026. It explicitly covers uneven inputs, but its compact brief supplies no concrete unequal-target-mass fixture. No full destination implementation audit was performed.
- Proposed treatment: after the equal-rank one-step example, add a deeper uneven-mass variant, then connect it to synchronization boundaries. Preserve title and stable identity. Include masks/weighted class-index cross-entropy after the scalar arithmetic is understood.
- Explanation/example: at scalar weight 0, half-squared losses for x=[1,2,3], y=[2,0,1] have per-item gradients [-2,0,-3]. Rank 0 holds the first two rows, rank 1 the third. Local mean gradients [-1,-3] average to -2, whereas global item mean is -5/3. Under default DDP averaging over W=2 ranks, each rank differentiates local summed numerator times W/D, with global D=3: local gradients [-4/3,-2] average to -5/3. For fixed nonnegative weights and masks, replace D by the summed eligible weight mass. Establish D across all ranks for the actual accumulation group.
- Prerequisites and boundaries: differentiation linearity, loss reduction, rank synchronization. All ranks must share compatible group boundaries and participate in required collectives, including a rank with zero local supervision while global mass is positive. Custom communication hooks, join behavior and changing world size require explicitly re-deriving the reduction convention. `no_sync` must include early-chunk forwards and backwards, followed by a synchronized pass. This proposal does not prescribe unsafely skipping one rank's backward.
- Evidence: [PyTorch 2.14 DDP](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.14/generated/torch.nn.parallel.DistributedDataParallel.html), class gradient-reduction notes and `no_sync()` section; [CrossEntropyLoss](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.14/generated/torch.nn.CrossEntropyLoss.html), class-index and probability-target reduction formulas. Relevant sections read 13 September 2026. Default rank averaging, forward coverage of no-sync, and target-weight denominator semantics checked. The formula is derived from that convention. CPU arithmetic is executed in the origin's `author_checks.py`, `author-results.json` key `ddp_arithmetic`; a distributed job has **not** been executed.
