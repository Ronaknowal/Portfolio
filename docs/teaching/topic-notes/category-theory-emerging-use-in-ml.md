## 2026-09-11 — Use homology as a concrete functor with meaningful information loss

- Status: adapted and implemented; author verification complete, independent review separate
- Origin: scoped design for [Topology & Topological Data Analysis](../TOPOLOGY-TDA-LESSON-DESIGN.md), the immediately preceding mathematics topic.
- Existing coverage inspected: the actual Category Theory body, sections 4–5, introduces functors through a qualified schema/pipeline analogy and gives the naturality equation. It does not give a worked homology functor or show how an inclusion can induce a noninjective map.
- Best owner: this topic can turn the learner's just-completed persistence mechanism into exact object/arrow/composition reasoning. TDA owns chains, boundaries and persistence; Category Theory owns why this construction is a functor and what that statement does and does not preserve.
- Proposed example: include the three-edge triangle into the filled triangle, then into a larger filled complex. Over F2, the edge cycle generates H1 of the first space and maps to zero in the filled triangle. The space inclusion is injective, but its induced H1 map is not. Homology respects identities and composition, not all properties of the original arrows. Work the boundary/quotient reason and an actual commuting diagram; do not imply that checking one diagram proves arbitrary functoriality.
- Learning benefit: distinguish an object's representation from the maps relating representations, and preservation of composition from preservation of all information. This supplies a precise counterexample to the misleading intuition that every structure-preserving map is injective or invertible.
- Evidence: Hatcher, [Algebraic Topology](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/AT.pdf), chapter 0 and section 2.1 selected pages, and ETH [persistent homology chapter](https://ti.inf.ethz.ch/ew/courses/TDA25/Chapter4.pdf), sections 4.2–4.3, actually inspected 10 September 2026 UTC during TDA design. The TDA author derived this finite F2 example; its final implementation/verification will supply the exact fixture. These readings are not a review of all categorical homology theory.
- Prerequisites and limits: recall the triangle chain and quotient locally. General functor laws require the induced-map construction, not just a numeric rank table. Do not silently require a prior algebraic topology course.
- Resolution: Category Theory section 3 now proves that a chain map carries cycles to cycles and boundaries to boundaries, hence induces a well-defined map on quotient homology; representatives establish identity and composition preservation. The concrete two-stage example is sufficient for the information-loss counterexample; the general representative proof supplies composition, rather than adding an otherwise redundant third drawing.
