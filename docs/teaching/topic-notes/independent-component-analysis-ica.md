# Authoring notes: Independent Component Analysis (ICA)

Canonical topic ID: independent-component-analysis-ica

## 2026-09-12 — Preserve the new separation mechanism and repair the original claims

- Status: resolved by the 2026-09-14 implementation below; the content-first treatment recorded here was consumed unchanged.
- Origin: full original-source assessment and [ICA design/research record](../ICA-LESSON-DESIGN.md).
- Destination and ownership rationale: ICA is the direct owner of independence versus decorrelation, whitening's remaining orthogonal freedom, identifiable source ambiguities and fixed-point constraints. PCA and embedding lessons can link this mechanism but should not acquire a duplicated ICA implementation.
- Idea and learning benefit: the learner should predict a distribution-sensitive change while covariance stays fixed, follow an exact FastICA step, and evaluate whether a recovered component helps an actual task.
- Existing coverage: original ICA JSX at commit 8c5da59f18516be77c29d5aeeafca3decca4f738 broadly mentions all these mechanisms, but wrongly claims equal non-Gaussian kurtoses create a flat ring, conflates PCA/ICA on Gaussian inputs, misstates MI optimization sign, and deflates only after convergence in displayed code. It also contains unsupported timing/sample/SNR thresholds and unverified source/causal interpretations. Published status is not review evidence.
- Proposed treatment: retain the stable title and broad coverage, use the exact binary four-state mixture, distinguish population and finite-sample statements, put deflation inside every iteration, include the zero-kurtosis non-Gaussian counterexample, and keep fixed source-vs-sensor conventions. Use the supplied real r01 recording to compare an explicit development-selected/held-out waveform diagnostic against raw and PCA baselines.
- Explanation/example: A=[[2,1],[1,2]], independent signs ±1 yield covariance[[5,4],[4,5]], whiten to a dependent diamond, then separate by an orthogonal change. Two standardized Laplace sources have projection kurtosis3(a⁴+b⁴), so equal kurtoses still give axis maxima. Their 30° projection is1.875. Full details, prediction contracts and exact calculation inputs are in the retained packet.
- Prerequisites and boundaries: dot products, covariance/eigenvectors and probability products, with local refreshers; later continuous objective branch introduces entropy/volume. No generic kurtosis cutoff proves Gaussianity. Simultaneous source independence differs from temporal sample independence. Physiological labels, actual clinical evaluation and convolutive audio need additional evidence/assumptions.
- Evidence: [Hyvärinen & Oja2000](https://www.cs.helsinki.fi/u/ahyvarin/papers/NN00new.pdf), especially §§2–6 and fixed-point/deflation equations; [HKO2001](https://www.cs.helsinki.fi/u/ahyvarin/papers/bookfinal_ICA.pdf) chapter7.4–7.5 and8.2.1 explicitly include the relevant whitening and equal-kurtosis mechanisms; [FastICA API](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.decomposition.FastICA.html). Sources reviewed12 September2026; detailed locators and actual review extent in the design.
- Resolution: assessed and fully written in [lesson manuscript](../drafts/independent-component-analysis-ica/lesson.md), [visual specifications](../drafts/independent-component-analysis-ica/visual-specifications.md), real data/provenance and author calculations. Phase two consumed that packet on 14 September 2026; the production evidence is linked in the section below. Do not rewrite the lesson from this short note.
- Implementation/verification links: elementary author evidence in the design at content phase; the phase-two model, native, data and browser evidence is linked in the 2026-09-14 section below. Independent review is still not started.

Related discoveries are saved by the integration owner against [NMF](non-negative-matrix-factorization-nmf.md), [neural preprocessing](neural-preprocessing-artifact-rejection-and-leakage-safe-pipelines.md), and [audio source separation](source-separation-audio-denoising-demucs-band-split-rnn.md).
