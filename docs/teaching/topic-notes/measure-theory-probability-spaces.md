# Authoring notes: Measure Theory & Probability Spaces

## 2026-09-10 — Give product-space integration a concrete geometric entry point

- Status: implemented
- Origin: [Multivariate Calculus & Gradients design](../MULTIVARIATE-CALCULUS-GRADIENTS-DESIGN.md), scoped inspection of calculus/measure/probability coverage.
- Destination and ownership rationale: this lesson explicitly lists product measures as a deeper branch and owns conditions under which integration is legitimate. It can connect rectangular area sums, joint density and iterated integrals before formal Fubini/Tonelli statements. A separate full computational multivariable-integration/vector-calculus sequence may ultimately be preferable; ownership beyond this bridge has not been audited.
- Idea and learning benefit: explain why an area element is dx dy, why integrating a joint density over one coordinate yields a marginal, and why swapping limits or integration order requires hypotheses. Coordinate changes must transform area/volume as well as the argument of the density; a Jacobian matrix is not itself an area multiplier.
- Existing coverage: current `measure-theory-probability-spaces.jsx` sections 5 and 7 give single-variable density/expectation and mention product measures; they do not work an explicit two-variable integral or its change-of-variables geometry. `single-variable-calculus-limits-derivatives-integrals` is an introductory single-variable plan. The new Multivariate lesson focuses on differentials/gradients and gives a small path-integral bridge; it does not teach area integrals. This was a narrow title/brief/source search, not a universal catalogue audit.
- Proposed treatment: add a small rectangular-cell-to-integral picture and a complete joint-density calculation before abstract product spaces; then state the nonnegative/integrable hypotheses for valid interchange. Reassess whether computational integration needs its own foundation owner rather than overloading this advanced page.
- Explanation/example: on the unit square, density p(x,y)=4xy is nonnegative and integrates to 1; its x marginal is 2x. Under u=2x, v=3y the rectangle becomes [0,2]×[0,3], and density must divide by the forward area scale 6 after substituting x=u/2, y=v/3. Compare cell mass before and after the transformation. Work and independently verify all bounds, normalizations and determinants when authoring.
- Prerequisites and boundaries: single-variable integration, joint densities, vectors/matrices, determinant meaning. Do not present Fubini as unconditional, or treat every nonlinear coordinate map as globally one-to-one. General surface flux/circulation and Stokes are outside this specific probability bridge.
- Evidence: current local source/brief inspection on 10 September 2026; [OpenStax Calculus 3 chapter 5](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-3/pages/5-introduction) is a primary textbook starting point for the receiving author. The proposed numeric example is an author-constructed exact case, not a reported experiment. Formal product-measure/change-of-variable sources should be checked during the destination rewrite.
- Resolution: implemented during the scoped Measure Theory rewrite on 10 September 2026. Section 7 derives the product rectangle measure, density normalization, marginal, exact 15/256 cell mass and coordinate density uv/9. Its dedicated joint-cell investigation preserves drawing units to show the area change, while the complete Fraction program calculates both integrals. Tonelli/Fubini conditions and the signed infinite-array counterexample explain when iterated integration is valid. No title or curriculum identity change was needed. General flux/circulation/Stokes remain outside this probability bridge.
