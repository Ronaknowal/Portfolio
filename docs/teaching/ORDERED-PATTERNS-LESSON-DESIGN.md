## Depth and accuracy decisions

Core: baseline scan; boundary invariant/termination; duplicates/absence; actual sorting APIs; stable insertion and merge; three-way partition reasoning; opposing/read-write/merge pointers; unique triple values; interval union; fixed and nonnegative variable windows; uniqueness windows; prefix/suffix cancellation and signed exact-sum counts; integer answer search; mixed independent synthesis. Deeper: comparison lower bound, heapsort as the constant-auxiliary alternative, counting/radix domain assumptions, randomized versus deterministic pivot costs, floating bisection termination, external ordered runs and dynamic-query ownership. Essential assumptions stay visible in core.
