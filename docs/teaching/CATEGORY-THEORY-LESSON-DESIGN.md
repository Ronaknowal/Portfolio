## Teaching flow and support

| Learning question | Complete explanation and example | Evidence of understanding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What can I connect, and what stays unchanged when I regroup it? | Small total finite functions with named source/target sets, followed by the original arithmetic composition program. Derive associativity pointwise and both identity laws. Keep the order of arrows fixed. | Predict an intermediate value, diagnose a type mismatch, and give a noncommuting pair. A finite check supports the fixture; the arbitrary-input proof establishes function laws. |
| What is a category beyond a flowchart? | Specify hom-sets and composition, then Set/FinSet, matrices, a preorder as a thin category, and a monoid as one-object category. A graph generates paths; path equality and imposed relations matter. A diagram commutes only if its parallel composites agree. | Construct the missing composites/identities, distinguish a path from its interpretation, and explain why reversing an arrow's drawn direction does not create an inverse. |
| When are two things interchangeable? | Two-sided inverse and uniqueness proof; one-sided retraction counterexample. Explain equality versus isomorphism, monic/epic cancellation with their Set meanings and category-relative caveat. | Check both inverse equations; show a projection has a section without being invertible. |
| How can data be an actual functor? | Source schema Sensor→Device→Site with a direct Sensor→Site arrow required to equal the composite. Instance assigns finite sets and total foreign-key functions. Spell out identity and composition preservation. Correct a bad direct-site value. | Identify a violating sensor, repair it, and explain why a missing/NULL foreign key is outside this total-function model. |
| What does a functor preserve or forget? | List on objects and pointwise map on arrows, with two functor-law proofs. Constant functor can lose information. Prove inverse preservation. Homology takes continuous/chain maps to induced linear maps: use the incoming triangle-edge→filled-triangle→larger-complex inclusion and explain the quotient's well-definedness. | Explain why injective space inclusion need not induce injective H1; trace a cycle representative and boundary under a chain map. Opposite categories reverse formal composition, not invert every morphism. |
| What makes a translation natural? | Define parallel F,G and every component before the square. Reverse:List⇒List commutes with any element map by an index proof. Sorting over ordered finite sets is a family of functions whose extension to all underlying maps fails naturality; use a reversing map as counterexample. Include a compatible schema-instance migration as an applied second reading. | Trace both routes on a changed list, distinguish successful examples from a universal law, and repair an incompatible migration. |
| How can a diagram specify what an object must do? | Product projections and unique pairing, terminal singleton, tagged coproduct and pullback as a compatible-pair join. Missing pair breaks existence; duplicated pair breaks uniqueness. Prove pairing and pullback factorization pointwise; uniqueness up to unique structure-preserving isomorphism follows from uniqueness of mediators. | Count mediators for changed cones; do not test a pullback against an incompatible cone as if the premise held. Show why ordinary union is not the disjoint coproduct when labels overlap. |
| How do parallel work and effects change composition? | Monoidal sequential/parallel interchange with typed wire diagram. Set product versus vector-space tensor; coherent associators are not literal equality of nested tuples. Option/Kleisli composition exposes failure and propagates it, with unit and associativity by cases. Introduce monad unit/flatten and laws through Option, not an unexplained programming slogan. | Follow both branches of a failure pipeline, compare composition with an unwrapped function, and diagnose illicit wire copying in a different category. |
| Why is copying a random result different from drawing twice? | Finite stochastic arrows use row-stochastic matrices and sum over the middle state. Compute sequential composition and product channels. One coin then deterministic copy gives a correlated diagonal joint; two independent coins give a product joint with the same marginals. Derive why arbitrary stochastic arrows do not commute with copying. Bayes reversal needs a prior and is not generally an inverse. | Calculate changed probabilities exactly, preserve normalization, find a joint from marginals is nonunique, and treat zero-evidence conditioning explicitly. |
| What must an automatic-differentiation interface carry? | Tangent transformation Tf(x,v)=(f(x),Df_x v) retains the input point. Chain rule proves composition and identity preservation. For f(x)=x+1 and g(x)=x² at0, discarding the evaluation point yields the wrong derivative0 instead of2. Show forward tangent and reverse cotangent paths with cached primal values; a complete two-layer/shared-parameter gradient example connects to learning. | Compare direct and composed derivatives at changed inputs, check a VJP pairing, and distinguish a derivative computation from a loss-decreasing optimizer guarantee. |
| What does an adjunction mean concretely? | Direct image and preimage between powerset posets: f(S)⊆T iff S⊆f⁻¹(T), proved by arbitrary elements. Establish monotonicity, unit/counit containments and information loss. General hom-set bijection and naturality are optional formal translation. | Find a failed containment witness on either side, derive the saturation of a selected set, and refute preservation of intersections by direct image. |
| What can all compatible probes reveal? | Contravariant h_A(X)=Hom(X,A), action by precomposition, and Nat(h_A,F)≅F(A). Derive a=θ_A(id_A), θ_X(u)=F(u)(a), check naturality and both inverse constructions. Work a tiny full finite subcategory with objects0,1,2 and all functions; distinguish universal compatible probes from arbitrary learned features. | Reconstruct a map from the identity component; explain why omitting compatibility or most probes invalidates a Yoneda analogy. Full native finite enumeration is bounded and optional, not browser work. |
| Can I audit a whole small system? | A sensor-data migration and compatible-pair allocation capstone, with explicit schema/laws, invalid case, repaired case, complete code and interpreted output. Changed practice also spans naturality, products, stochastic copy and derivative interfaces. | Independent exercises have hints and explained answers, and computational answers have complete runnable solutions or explicit acceptance cases. Readiness tests reasoning and changed inputs, not terminology recall. |
