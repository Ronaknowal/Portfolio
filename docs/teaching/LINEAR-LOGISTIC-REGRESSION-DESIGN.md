## Learning contract and sequence

An observation is a past parcel shipment. Features are information known when estimating delivery; the continuous target is elapsed hours and the binary target is whether a stated deadline was missed. Use synthetic units and tiny exact data, with no claim of carrier performance. The learner should distinguish fitting a numerical prediction from a probability, manually compute residuals/losses and updates, diagnose rank/separation/leakage, evaluate a held-out example against a baseline and complete a small reproducible pipeline.

The core first defines observation/feature/target, training/validation/test and prediction-time availability. It then connects a line to residuals and a mean baseline; derives one-dimensional OLS and feature-matrix least squares; exposes a parameter update; maps score→odds→probability→decision; derives stable log loss and its gradient; separates training from threshold choice and evaluation; and supplies complete pipelines. Deeper branches cover rank/regularization, nonlinear feature maps, uncertainty/influence and model/solver scaling. Mathematical foundations are linked, but local notation and essential evaluation/scaling bridges are self-contained. The later Problem Formulation and Metrics owners remain fuller treatments, not mandatory unexplained detours.
