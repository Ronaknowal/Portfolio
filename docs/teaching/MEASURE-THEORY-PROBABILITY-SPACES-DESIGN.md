## Scope and preservation

Retain the title: it accurately promises the probability-space, integration and information foundations now developed. Preserve the fair-die example and its executed mean/event calculations, random variables as measurable maps, induced laws, mass-versus-density, expected ML loss, conditional information/projection, independence caveats and links to deeper stochastic theory.

Repair two original overstatements: a probability measure can exist on every subset of an uncountable space (a Dirac measure is a simple example); the relevant obstruction concerns extending length with translation invariance and countable additivity. The minimum-squared-error interpretation of conditional expectation needs square-integrability and equality only up to null sets. General integrable conditional expectation has a defining event-integral property independent of that extra assumption.

Prior skills: elementary sets/functions, sums, probability/expectation and one-variable integrals. The recorded Real Analysis supporting topic is later in module order, not a reason to reorder this lesson: supply local explanations of countable versus uncountable, increasing sets, pointwise/almost-everywhere limits and integration notation. Probability15 and Bayesian18 provide distribution/conditioning context; the preceding Exponential Families lesson22 motivates densities and sufficient information. Next is Optimal Transport24, whose couplings and pushforwards use this language.

Beginner finish line: construct a finite probability space, identify events made observable by a partition, trace a random variable's preimage and law, distinguish point mass/density/general measure, compute expectation and a conditional mean. Intermediate: justify a product/marginal integral, preserve mass under a coordinate scale, test a limit interchange and reweight by a valid density ratio. Deeper: Borel/completion/length obstruction, singular atomless probability, MCT/DCT/Fubini hypotheses, conditional expectation uniqueness/tower/projection and Radon–Nikodym interpretation. This is not a full graduate measure-theory book or a proof of every extension theorem.

## Proof and numerical contracts

- Define measures, measurability and null sets relative to a specified sigma-algebra/measure. A zero-probability event need not be impossible, and a null set need not be empty. Borel and completed Lebesgue sigma-algebras are not interchangeable without a stated completion.
- Prove finite partition equivalence, pushforward identities, event continuity and conditional-projection decomposition through intermediate steps. Give an accessible explanation before any quantified theorem.
- State countable additivity, not arbitrary uncountable sums. Infinite visual sequences are finite prefixes whose limiting claim needs a proof.
- Nonnegative expectations may equal+∞. For ordinary finite signed expectation require E|Y|<∞; avoid inventing an expectation from principal-value cancellation.
- MCT requires nonnegative increasing measurable functions; DCT requires an integrable dominating function and a.e. convergence. Show why the failed spike meets neither useful condition. Distinguish a fixed x from an n-dependent location.
- Product statements use sigma-finite reference measures. Tonelli permits+∞ for nonnegative integrands; Fubini's finite signed conclusion uses absolute integrability. Independence means joint law equals product of marginals, not merely that the domain is rectangular.
- The coordinate example is an invertible positive diagonal linear map with absolute determinant6. General differentiable changes require appropriate one-to-one/local-branch conditions; do not infer that every transform has a single inverse or nonsingular Jacobian.
- Conditional expectation is a G-measurable integrable version matching all G-event integrals, unique P-a.s. A zero-mass cell has no uniquely forced displayed value. The L² prediction statement uses Y and candidate predictors in L².
- RN theorem is stated with suitable sigma-finiteness and absolute continuity; density means a measure derivative relative to a reference, not a point probability. General theorem proof may be linked; demonstrate finite reweighting and the conditional-expectation existence connection.

Native examples are complete Python programs, mostly exact Fraction/enumeration where that is the appropriate evidence. Use NumPy/SciPy only where quadrature or linear-algebra comparison materially improves verification. All printed outputs must be executed. The browser models are bounded teaching calculations, not estimators from undisclosed measurements.

Independent practice should change actual data or assumptions: generate a new finite information algebra, build a preimage law, distinguish a mixed atom/interval, construct an integrable versus divergent signed expectation, analyze n^a1_(0,n^-b), transform a different rectangle, calculate a changed conditional prediction and diagnose missing support in a density ratio. Supply hints, complete reasoning and acceptance criteria; include the original classifier-space synthesis with explicit sampling unit and possible dependence.
