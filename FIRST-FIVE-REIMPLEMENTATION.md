Complete dataclass/default/equality examples and an independent DatasetSplit invariant task; inheritance, method kinds and MRO are progressively disclosed. |

These are **15 focused interactive labs**, plus static diagrams, tables and complete programs. The count follows distinct conceptual hurdles. It is not a quota for future lessons. Core explanations introduce vocabulary and syntax before depending on them; optional sections preserve depth. Predictions precede reveals, controls change meaningful causes, feedback explains consequences, and independent tasks require transfer beyond reproducing the walkthrough.

The initial File/SQL review led to stricter error-gate labels, explicit missing/zero/all-missing behavior, source-row conversion errors and small syntax bridges. CSV's exact-empty-field convention is deliberately distinguished from the preceding Python report's whitespace-stripping rule. SQL's compact aggregate labels keep every result column visible on mobile; the adjacent legend maps them back to COUNT and AVG.
