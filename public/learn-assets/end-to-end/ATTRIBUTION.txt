wine.csv holds the unchanged bytes of the offline extract prepared for this curriculum,
12100 bytes, SHA-256 34ced17cfa0a96bf5ae5c25565da075c612145b20368fc2455e5e8f966e818be.

What the data are: chemical analyses of 178 wine specimens grown by three cultivars in the
same region of Italy. The task in this lesson is recognising the recorded cultivar of a
specimen from its measurements. It is not taste scoring, quality grading or clinical data,
and the class codes are names rather than an ordering.

Offline source: the bundled data of scikit-learn 1.9.1 `load_wine()`, documented at
https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.datasets.load_wine.html
No UCI archive was downloaded successfully while this packet was prepared, so no byte
comparison against the original archive is claimed.

Changes made to produce this file: the numeric measurements were written with %.8g
formatting; the source row order was kept; a one-based specimen_id column was added;
scikit-learn's feature names were used as column headings; and its zero-based class labels
were stored as cultivar = 0, 1, 2, corresponding to UCI's labels 1, 2 and 3. No row was
filtered, no value was imputed, no scaling was written into the file, and no missing value
was introduced. specimen_id and cultivar are never model features.

Comma separated, CRLF line endings, a header row and 178 data rows of fifteen columns:
specimen_id, then the thirteen measurements alcohol, malic_acid, ash, alcalinity_of_ash,
magnesium, total_phenols, flavanoids, nonflavanoid_phenols, proanthocyanins,
color_intensity, hue, od280/od315_of_diluted_wines and proline, then cultivar. All 178
specimen_id values are distinct and every measurement is finite. The three cultivar codes
hold 59, 71 and 48 specimens. The inspected UCI table does not state a unit for each
measurement, so this lesson names none and its figures use the recorded source scale.

What this lesson does with it: a stratified holdout of 36 specimens is taken first with
random_state 21, then a stratified validation set of 36 is taken from the remaining 142 with
random_state 22, leaving 106 training specimens. The three splits are disjoint and together
cover all 178 rows. Their cultivar counts are 35/43/28 for training and 12/14/10 for each of
validation and test. All four candidate models are fitted on the 106 training rows only. The
selected already-fitted model is evaluated once on the 36 test rows without refitting, and
the interactive development explorer on this page is restricted to the 36 validation rows.

Three other lessons in this curriculum serve wine measurements under
public/learn-assets/bayesian-networks/, public/learn-assets/pca/ and
public/learn-assets/feature-selection/. This lesson serves its own copy so that no other
lesson's asset edit can change what this page teaches, and it never references theirs. The
bayesian-networks copy is byte-identical to this one today; that is a fact about today, not
