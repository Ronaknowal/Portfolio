The extract is NumPy default_rng(23).permutation(1372) taken in that order, first 480 rows,
each carrying its one-based source_row identifier from the original file. No measurement was
transformed, no row was filtered and no column was dropped. Rows 1 to 320 of the file are the
split name "pool", rows 321 to 400 are "development" and rows 401 to 480 are "test". All 480
source_row identifiers are distinct and the three splits are disjoint.

Comma separated, CRLF line endings, a header row and 480 data rows of seven columns:
source_row, split, variance, skewness, curtosis, entropy, class. The source spelling
"curtosis" is retained. The four measurements span variance -6.7387 to 6.5633, skewness
-13.6779 to 12.7302, curtosis -5.2861 to 17.6052 and entropy -7.7853 to 2.1353. The class
column holds 275 zeros and 205 ones over the whole file, and 41 zeros and 39 ones over the
80 development rows.

"class" is the source's own binary class code. This curriculum does not assign it a
genuine-or-forged meaning, because the UCI record does not state which code is which.

What this lesson does with it: all 320 pool rows are labelled training data. A fixed
permutation of those 320 row positions, NumPy default_rng(44), defines nested training
prefixes of 20, 40, 80, 160 and 320 examples. Three predeclared procedures are fitted at each
size and scored on their own training prefix and on the fixed 80 development rows. The 80
test rows are NOT evaluated by this lesson's program, and no final selected-model estimate is
reported from them.
