wine.csv holds the unchanged bytes of the offline extract prepared for this curriculum,
12100 bytes, SHA-256 34ced17cfa0a96bf5ae5c25565da075c612145b20368fc2455e5e8f966e818be.
That extract was produced with scikit-learn 1.9.1 load_wine, exported with %.8g formatting,
preserving the source row order, adding a specimen_id column numbered 1 to 178, and naming
the zero-based target column cultivar with values 0, 1 and 2 (the UCI record labels the same
three cultivars 1, 2 and 3). No row was filtered, no measurement was transformed and no
column was dropped: all thirteen original measurements are retained here even though this
lesson reads only four of them. No fresh byte comparison against the original UCI archive
is claimed; the recorded SHA-256 above pins exactly what this page serves.

Comma separated, CRLF line endings, a header row and 178 data rows of fifteen columns:
specimen_id, then the thirteen measurements alcohol, malic_acid, ash, alcalinity_of_ash,
magnesium, total_phenols, flavanoids, nonflavanoid_phenols, proanthocyanins,
color_intensity, hue, od280/od315_of_diluted_wines and proline, then cultivar.
All 178 rows are distinct. The three cultivars hold 59, 71 and 48 specimens.
The specimens are chemical analyses of wines grown in one region of Italy by three
different cultivators. This is cultivar recognition. It is not wine quality, taste,
price, intervention or clinical outcome data, and no collection date, vintage,
sub-region or grower identifier accompanies the record.

Measurement units are not supplied by the source record and are not invented here.
The four columns this lesson reads span alcohol 11.03 to 14.83, malic_acid 0.74 to 5.80,
flavanoids 0.34 to 5.08 and color_intensity 1.28 to 13.00.
