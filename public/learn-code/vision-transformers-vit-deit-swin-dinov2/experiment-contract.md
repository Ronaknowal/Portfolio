# Declared small vision study

Declared 13 September 2026 before fitting. Content-only author calculation; implementation remains pending.

Use the unchanged UCI Optical Recognition of Handwritten Digits original train/test files, attributed to E. Alpaydin and C. Kaynak (1998), CC BY 4.0, DOI 10.24432/C50P49. The official collection has separate 30-writer training and 13-writer test pools; individual writer IDs are not present. All 3,823 training vectors and 1,797 test vectors are unique, with no cross-pool exact vector matches or conflicting duplicate labels. Select 120 training and 30 validation rows per digit from the original training pool with NumPy default_rng(173), in class order. Other original training rows remain unused. Use the full original test pool once after selecting checkpoints. This is a small educational protocol, not the original full-training benchmark.

Inputs are one-channel 8×8 counts divided by the fixed published upper bound 16. No fitted scaling, augmentation or pretrained weights. Compare three models: small CNN teacher; two-block ViT with 2×2 patches, width32, four heads and FFN width64; identical shared initialization with an added distillation token, positional slot and classification head. CNN: Conv1→12 k3p1, ReLU, MaxPool2, Conv12→24 k3p1, ReLU, flatten384→32, ReLU, head32→10. The plain ViT uses CLS cross-entropy. The distilled student uses half label cross-entropy at CLS plus half cross-entropy against frozen CNN argmax labels at DIST; inference averages the two logits. This isolates a small DeiT-style mechanism, not the full DeiT recipe or a controlled parameter-equal architecture comparison.

Each model gets 600 AdamW updates at learning rate .002, weight decay .01 on all parameters, batch128 sampled with replacement from the same seed301 index stream. Initialize the CNN with seed211 and plain ViT with seed223. Copy every common ViT tensor into the distilled model; initialize its extra DIST token/position to zero and copy its head from the plain CLS head. No dropout, stochastic depth, mixed precision, augmentation or learning-rate schedule. Measure validation cross-entropy every50 steps and at step0; choose the smallest value, ties to the earliest step. Test only after this rule selects the checkpoint. Teacher predictions used for distillation come from its selected frozen evaluation state. Keep every outcome; do not tune until the student wins. One CPU thread, modest float32 models. Save state, split IDs, results and first three test specimens for deterministic later visual investigations. No wall-clock or foundation-model conclusions follow.

For frozen-feature comparison, fit a training-only StandardScaler+LogisticRegression(C=1,max_iter=2000) on raw pixels and on each selected network's clean feature representation; validation and test are transformed only. These readouts are additional bounded fits, declared here, and answer representation/readout questions rather than asserting DINO pretraining was performed. Record the network's original head beside its linear readout; do not call these probes independent tests of self-supervised learning.
